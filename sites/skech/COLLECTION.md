# Skech

Source: https://github.com/skech-trade/monorepo
Original app path: ui/landing

From the collection root: `bun install`, then `bun run --cwd sites/skech dev --port 3102`.

The collection excludes backend services, contracts, private configuration and original research captures. Live product links remain references to their respective projects. Replace branding and connect your own services before using as a new product.

Unverified bitmap artwork and third-party brand graphics are replaced with original procedural sample art. Audio and original video assets are omitted.
